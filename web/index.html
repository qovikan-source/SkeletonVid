Frequently Asked Questions

Why does SkeletonVid Pro use a small companion app?
Chrome restricts extensions from writing large files directly to disk or merging HD audio and video without consuming gigabytes of browser RAM. Our 6 MB native companion runs only while downloading, giving you 5x faster speeds and zero browser lag.
Will this work on private courses I have paid for and logged into?
Yes. Because the Chrome extension runs inside your authenticated browser session, it forwards the required stream manifest and session headers directly to the companion app on your machine—your credentials never leave your computer.
Can it download DRM-protected streams like Netflix?
No. SkeletonVid Pro respects hardware and software Digital Rights Management (Widevine, FairPlay, PlayReady). If a video uses EME encryption, the extension notifies you immediately.
